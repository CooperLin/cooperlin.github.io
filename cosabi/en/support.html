Support & FAQ

App Instructions

How to use this App?

Cosabi is a personal item management tool that helps you remember the status of everything in your home—including location, purchase price, expiration dates, warranty, inventory levels, and historical price comparisons.

You can use it in three main ways:

1. Add Items
Tap the "Add" tab, take a photo of an item, and the AI will automatically identify and pre-fill the name, expiration date, and price. Save it to your warehouse after confirmation.

2. Record Market Prices
Tap "Record Price" in the Add tab. Point your camera at a price tag, and OCR will automatically recognize the product name and price for quick comparison.

3. Query Items
In the "Search" tab, use voice or text to find items, check spending history, or filter items that are about to expire.

1. Why is the AI pre-filled information incorrect?

Cosabi uses on-device AI models to analyze photos. Accuracy depends on lighting, angles, and packaging design. If the results are unsatisfactory, you can edit them directly on the confirmation screen; only your final confirmation is saved.

Tip: Try to align the item frontally, ensure the image is clear, and have sufficient lighting.

2. What is the info card that appears on the camera screen?

When the camera detects text labels (name/price) or recognizes an item already in your warehouse, an info card will float at the bottom showing the name, last purchase price, and inventory.

Tap the arrow on the card to see the full price trend. The card won't appear if no relevant content is detected.

3. What is a "Market Price Record" vs. a "Warehouse Item"?

A "Market Price Record" is a quick note of a price you saw at a store, which doesn't necessarily have to be an item you own.

If you want to link a price record to a warehouse item later, find the record in the "Search" page and tap "Link." Once linked, it will be included in that item's price trend chart.

4. How do I check how much I've spent on an item?

In the "Search" tab, say "How much did [item name] cost?" or type the name to see a timeline of all related purchases, showing sub-totals and the total amount spent.

5. When are notifications sent?

If an item has an expiration date or replacement cycle, Cosabi sends reminders 7 days before, 1 day before, and on the day of expiry. Warranty reminders follow the same rule.

Please ensure notifications are allowed in your device settings and enabled in the App settings.

6. What is included in the backup? Do I need to restart after restoring?

The backup file (.cosabi) includes all warehouse data, search indices, and photos. You must manually restart the App after restoring to apply the content.

It is recommended to export a backup before switching phones or reinstalling the App.

7. Can I recover deleted items?

Currently, deletion is permanent. Please proceed with caution and back up your data regularly in the settings page.
