# Developer's Note: From a Father's "Goldfish Brain" I am a **Christian**, an **independent software developer**, and a father of three (oh, and a cat). Back when I was single and under my parents' protection, life's little details never seemed to be an issue. But since starting a family and moving out, reality has hit hard. I don't just have to manage piles of household supplies; I also have to track seasonal price fluctuations to fight inflation... for my limited brain capacity, this was a massive challenge. ### Admitting the Problem is the First Step to Solving It Before writing the first line of code, I honestly faced my two biggest pain points: 1. **Spatial Disorientation**: I truly couldn't remember which corner things were tucked into, or how much was left. 2. **Price Disconnection**: I couldn't instantly recall the price of the same item from three months ago while standing in the supermarket. So, my "Developer Persona" awakened: If my brain can't remember it, let the digital tools handle it! --- ## Why AI? In this era of AI explosion, edge computing and Embedding models have made development full of possibilities. I believe that **a tool shouldn't just be a cold database; it should be an extension of your brain**. Through AI's semantic integration, this App bridges the "cognitive gap" between user input and querying. You don't need to remember precise keywords for every item; the AI builds a bridge, making information retrieval as natural as talking to an old friend. We no longer have to struggle alone; by standing on the shoulders of giants, life can be a bit more elegant. --- ## Development Journey: Purity After Subtraction The initial plan was grand. I ambitiously wanted to include "Invoice Carrier" features, attempting to build a "Super App" connecting warehouse, prices, and finances. However, during the technical research phase, I hit a real-world wall—government restrictions on API access for independent developers. This essentially broke one of the original pillars of the project. **But because of this "accident," I re-examined the essence of development.** I decided to return to the core and stop chasing feature accumulation. By removing complex integrations, I was able to focus on optimizing the fluency of "Warehouse Management" and "Price Recording." This imperfect regret ultimately transformed into a design of simplicity and peace of mind. ### Our Core Spirit * **Warehouse**: Clearly master household supplies and say goodbye to duplicate purchases. * **Price Tracking**: Digitally track your living expenses and master price patterns. * **AI Integration**: Make querying a breeze instead of a chore. --- ## Final Words This App was designed for people like me—those striving to manage a household and pursue efficiency amidst a busy life. It might not be perfect, but it is authentic and practical. I hope this little tool lightens your load even just a bit. If you have any suggestions during use, please feel free to reach out. Let's make this App better together! **May God bless your every day.**
