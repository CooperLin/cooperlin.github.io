# Privacy Policy **Updated:** November 20, 2026 **Applicable App:** Cosabi **Cosabi**, developed by Cooper Lin ("we," "us," or "our"), is a privacy-focused "Tracking" and "Price" logging tool built around local storage. This policy explains how we handle data so you can record the time and prices of everyday life with peace of mind. ## 1. Core Privacy Principle: Your Data Stays on Your Phone Everything you create in the App, including tracked items, check-ins, notes, priced items and prices, tags, places (stores), and the photos you take or choose, is **stored only in your device's local storage**. - **No account needed:** Cosabi requires no sign-up or login. - **No uploads:** We do not upload any of the above to a server, and we cannot access, restore, or back it up for you. - **Changing phones:** If you change phones, use the App's backup and restore feature to move your data yourself (see Section 4). ## 2. Permissions | Permission | Purpose | |------------|---------| | **Camera** | Take photos of price tags and items. Text on price tags is recognized by Apple Vision **on your device; neither the photo nor the recognition leaves your device**. | | **Photo Library** | Pick photos from your library to attach to records. | | **Notifications** | Send tracking reminders at the time you set. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not pass through our servers. | You can turn off any permission in system Settings at any time without affecting other features. ## 3. Data We Collect To improve the product and fix bugs, we collect only the following two kinds of data through Google's Firebase, **neither of which contains your personal content**. ### A. Usage Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) - **What:** How often and in what flow features are used, for example which screen was opened, whether adding an item was completed, whether photo recognition found text, which price unit was chosen, and whether the camera and notification permissions are enabled. - **Not included:** Item names, prices, notes, tags, place names, and photos you enter are never sent. - **Device info:** Device model, OS version, App version, and an anonymous App instance ID generated by Firebase. ### B. Crash and Error Reports (Firebase Crashlytics) - **What:** The stack trace and device state when the App crashes, and logs of non-fatal errors such as a failed save. - **Purpose:** Used only to find and fix problems. All of this data is anonymous, and we cannot use it to identify you. Cosabi shows no ads and does not track you across apps. ## 4. Backup and Restore - Backups are created by you inside the App. The file is encrypted with **AES-256-GCM** and needs the password you set in order to restore. - **Only you know the password.** We cannot reset or unlock it. A backup whose password is lost cannot be restored. - The backup file stays on your device. Whether you share it or store it in the cloud is entirely up to you. ## 5. Third-Party Services | Service | Function | Privacy Note | |---------|----------|--------------| | **Google Analytics for Firebase** | Anonymous usage statistics | See Section 3 A | | **Firebase Crashlytics** | Crash and error reports | See Section 3 B | | **GitHub Pages / jsDelivr** | When you open "Privacy Policy," "Terms of Service," or "Developer's Note" in Settings, the App loads the page content and a rendering component from here | These services may log your IP address under their standard practices while a page is open; the App does not connect to them during normal use | Cosabi currently has **no paid features or in-app purchases**, and uses no AI or cloud recognition service. ## 6. Your Rights and Age Restrictions - **Data deletion:** Because your data is stored locally, simply deleting the App removes all related data from your device. If you also want to remove exported backup files, delete them separately. - **Age:** This App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from minors. ## 7. Changes to This Policy If future changes affect how data is handled (for example, adding cloud or AI features), we will update this policy and its date before offering those features. ## 8.
