# Privacy Policy **Updated:** June 1, 2026 **Applicable App:** Cosabi **Cosabi**, developed by Cooper Lin ("we," "us," or "our"), is a privacy-focused application centered on local storage. This policy explains how we handle data so you can manage your household supplies and price information with peace of mind. ## 1. Core Privacy Principle: Local Storage We adhere to a **"Privacy First"** principle. Your core data (including but not limited to: item names, inventory counts, purchase prices, notes, and AI-generated vector data) is **stored locally on your mobile device**. - **Data Sovereignty:** We do not upload your warehouse data to any cloud servers. - **Data Migration:** If you change phones, you must use the export/import function provided within the App to transfer your data. We cannot access, restore, or back up your local data. ## 2. Data Collection and Purpose To optimize the product experience and maintain operations, we only collect two types of non-sensitive information: ### A. Usage Behavior and Error Logs (GA4 & Firebase) We use third-party tools from Google to understand feature usage frequency (e.g., which features are most popular, AI query success rates, etc.). - **Data Types:** Device model, OS version, app launch frequency, feature clicks, and crash reports. - **Anonymization:** This data is processed anonymously and cannot be traced back to any specific individual. ### B. Purchase and Payment Information (Apple App Store) When you purchase the Premium features, all transaction details (such as credit card numbers and billing addresses) are handled by **Apple Inc.** - **Developer Access:** We only receive a confirmation token indicating whether the purchase is valid. We **cannot** access your real name or any payment details. ## 3. AI Technology and Data Security The AI features embedded in the App (such as semantic search and Embeddings) primarily run locally or within encrypted transmission environments. - **No Tracking:** Your query content is only used to build a retrieval index. We do not collect your search terms for advertising purposes or to train general AI models. ## 4. Third-Party Service Providers We collaborate with the following services to enhance performance and operations; their privacy practices follow their respective platform standards: | Service Provider | Function | Privacy Disclosure | |------------------|----------|-------------------| | **GA4 for Firebase** | Usage statistics and analysis | Anonymous behavior tracking | | **Apple In-App Purchase** | One-time Premium purchase | Protected by Apple Privacy | ## 5. User Rights and Age Restrictions - **Data Deletion:** Since data is stored locally, simply deleting the App will permanently remove all associated data from your device. - **Age Limits:** This App is suitable for all ages. We do not intentionally collect any data from minors. ## 6.
