# Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** November 23, 2026 **Applicable App:** Let's Bike Cooper Lin ("we," "us," or "our") developed the Let's Bike application. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information. ## 1. Information We Collect and Use This application does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address. The following describes the data the app handles and why. ### 1.1 Data Stored Only on Your Device - **Location (including background location):** Used to show nearby YouBike stations and public toilets, record your ride path, distance and speed, and detect the start and end of a ride automatically. Ride records (route, time, distance) are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or Firebase. To convert coordinates into an address or city, the app uses Apple's geocoding service; the coordinates are sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. - **Health data (HealthKit):** With your permission, the app writes your riding distance and calories to the Health app, and reads your health data as a basis for estimating calories. This data is used only for these features. It is not used for advertising and is not sent to our servers or any third party. - **Notifications:** With your permission, the app delivers ride-related reminders as local notifications on your device. ### 1.2 Motion & Fitness Data (Aggregated Statistics Only) With your permission for Motion & Fitness, the app reads, at the end of each ride, your Motion & Fitness data from before and after that ride (step counts and the accumulated time of activity states such as walking, running and cycling). It uploads **aggregated statistics** of this data, together with time and distance statistics of the ride, through Google Analytics for Firebase. This is used to analyze and improve the accuracy of riding-time detection. The uploaded data does not include location coordinates, routes, your name, or any other personally identifiable information. If you decline the permission, the rest of the app continues to work normally. ### 1.3 Anonymous Data Collected Through Third-Party Services We collect anonymized data through third-party services, including but not limited to: - Device model, operating system version and app version - Crash and error logs (for debugging and analysis purposes) - Screen views and feature usage statistics - A random app instance identifier generated by Firebase - Advertising identifier (IDFA): used to deliver personalized ads only if you choose "Allow" in the system App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt ## 2. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing This app uses the following third-party services. These services may collect anonymized device information to provide advertising and analytics features: | Third-Party Service | Purpose | Platform | |---------------------|----------|----------| | Google Firebase | App analytics and crash reporting | All platforms | | Google Analytics for Firebase | Usage statistics and traffic analysis | All platforms | | Google AdMob | Ad delivery and performance tracking | All platforms | | Apple Ads (Search Ads) | Ad impressions and click tracking | App Store | We do not sell or share your data with unauthorized third parties. All data processing complies with applicable privacy regulations, including GDPR and the Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. ## 3. Your Choices and Rights - You can turn off the app's Location, Motion & Fitness, Health, Notifications and Tracking permissions at any time in iOS Settings. - Ride records are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Data already written to the Health app is managed within the Health app. - Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' data retention settings and is deleted automatically after that period. If you have questions about data that has been uploaded, please contact us and we will do our best to help. ## 4. Data Security We implement industry-standard security measures through platforms such as Firebase to protect data. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. ## 5. Age Suitability This application is designed for users of all ages, particularly those who enjoy riding YouBike. ## 6.
